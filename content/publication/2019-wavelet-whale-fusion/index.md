abstract: |
  Image fusion technology can effectively improve the utilization ratio of image information, the accuracy of target recognition and the ability of image interpretation. In this paper, Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA) based on the fusion techniques with multiscale geometric analysis is applied into image fusion. We use wavelet decomposition for image preprocessing, and then present an approach of image fusion based on wavelet with WOA. Experiment results demonstrate that the proposed method has advantages in subjectively qualitative analysis and objectively quantitative evaluation compared with the other state of the art methods.
