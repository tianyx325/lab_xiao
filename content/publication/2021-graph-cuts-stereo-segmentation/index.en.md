abstract: |
  Stereo image segmentation is essential for subsequent applications such as stereo object composition and three-dimensional reconstruction. Since stereo images contain scene depth information, directly applying monocular image segmentation methods to stereo images cannot achieve ideal segmentation results. Currently, most stereo image segmentation methods use the depth features of stereo images merely as additional channels of the color features, integrating color and depth features in a simple manner without fully exploiting the depth information. Based on the multi-class Graph Cuts framework, this paper proposes an interactive stereo image segmentation method. The method fuses color, depth, and texture features into a single graph model to make fuller use of different feature information. Meanwhile, a feature-space neighborhood system is introduced into the Graph Cuts framework, which enhances the relationships among pixels within the foreground and background regions and improves the integrity of the segmented objects. Experimental results show that the proposed method effectively improves the accuracy of stereo image segmentation.
