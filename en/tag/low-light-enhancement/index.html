Low-Light Enhancement

Low-Light Vision Enhancement
Addressing low illumination and complex lighting, this direction studies image/video enhancement, denoising, and feature representation, improving the usability of downstream tasks such as focusing, matching, and reconstruction under low-light conditions.
PUB2026· JVCIR DOI Code
Reflectance oriented diffusion with normalizing flow illumination enhancement for the low-light images
Enhances illumination with a normalizing flow, restores reflectance detail with a diffusion model, and corrects saturation after recombination, balancing enhancement and noise suppression.
PUB2026· ICASSP DOI Code
FEDD-Net: A Frequency Diagonal Feature Enhanced Dual-Branch Diffusion Network for Low-Light Image Enhancement
Separates high/low frequencies with dual-tree complex wavelets, applies cross-attention across directions in the frequency domain to enhance diagonal features, and generates low-frequency structure and high-frequency detail via two diffusion paths.
PUB2026· Multimedia Systems DOI
A dual-guide aware normalizing flow for low-light enhancement with global illumination and cross-channel attention
Proposes a dual-guided normalizing flow that encodes illumination and color as separate conditions, mitigating uneven lighting and color distortion in low-light enhancement.

Custom Low-Light Image Enhancement Algorithm Development

Zhaolin Xiao

Customized low-light image enhancement algorithms for practical application scenarios, restoring visible details under low illumination to improve downstream recognition and analysis.

CEDFlow++: Latent Contour Enhancement for Dark Optical Flow Estimation
Adds factorized feature encoding, dual cost-volume inference, and contour-guided attention on top of CEDFlow to handle large-scale degradations in low light.
PUB2025· ICASSP DOI Code
Diffusion Model with Multi-layer Wavelet Transform for Low-Light Image Enhancement
Replaces convolutional up/downsampling with multi-level wavelet transforms to extract multi-scale features with global refinement in diffusion models for low-light enhancement.
PUB2024· ICIP DOI
A Cnn-Transformer Network Based Snr Guided High Frequency Reconstruction for Low Light Image Enhancement
Splits low-light images into high- and low-frequency bands and uses a low-frequency SNR map to guide convolution and Transformer recovery of high-frequency textures.
PUB2024· ICANN DOI
EDAFormer: Enhancing Low-Light Images with a Dual-Attention Transformer
Fuses global and local attention recursively and adds multi-scale context convolutional self-attention to enhance low-light images.
PUB2024· IJCNN DOI
A Multi-Exposure Generation and Fusion Method for Low-Light Image Enhancement
Generates images at multiple exposures following multi-exposure distributions, then fuses features with perceptual importance and curve adjustment to enhance low-light images.
PUB2024· JVCIR DOI
Learn to enhance the low-light image via a multi-exposure generation and fusion method
Generates multiple exposures from a single low-light image, selects well-exposed features by perceptual importance, and applies curve adjustment.
PUB2024· AAAI Paper Code
CEDFlow: Latent Contour Enhancement for Dark Optical Flow Estimation
Encodes motion in latent space with spatial–frequency decomposition and filters large-scale contours with second-order difference of Gaussians to estimate low-light optical flow.
