Saliency Detection

PUB2022· Computer Technology and Development Paper
A Survey of Salient Object Detection: Theory and Applications
Theoretical directions, major applications, and open problems of salient object detection are surveyed, noting that traditional methods are mainly limited to single-modality data in simple scenes.
PUB2021· Journal of Beijing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics Paper
Video Summarization Based on Multi-Feature Visual Saliency of Images
Superpixels and dual-contrast center-surround saliency are combined with optical flow and perceptual hashing to extract key-frame summaries from videos.
