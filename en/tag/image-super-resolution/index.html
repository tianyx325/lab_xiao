ZEPI-Net: Light Field Super Resolution via Internal Cross-Scale Epipolar Plane Image Zero-Shot Learning

EPI-Based Super-Resolution Method for Light Field Images
A method for super-resolving light field images based on epipolar plane image (EPI) structures, enhancing spatial resolution and edge fidelity.

A generative adversarial network-based method for axial super-resolution of light field images, improving detail sharpness and perceptual quality of refocused results.
