Action Recognition

Human Action Recognition
Focusing on human behavior understanding, this direction studies skeleton-based and visual-feature-based action recognition methods, covering spatio-temporal modeling, robust representation, and behavior understanding applications in real-world scenarios.
PUB2022· Journal of Image and Graphics Paper PDF
A Spliced Feature Matrix Learning Method for Open Field Test Video Classification
Behavioral features are extracted from open field videos and concatenated with motion path maps into matrices, which are then classified by convolutional neural networks to distinguish two groups of mice.
PAT2021· Granted
Methods for Extracting Multi-Dimensional Features from Mouse Videos and Classifying Videos
Methods for extracting multi-dimensional features from mouse videos and classifying them, supporting quantitative behavioral assessment and automatic video classification for laboratory animal behavior analysis.
PUB2021· Multimedia Tools and Applications DOI
Behavioral features fusion for ethological CNN classification of open field test videos
Extracts behavioral features from the spatial and temporal domains of open-field videos into 2D feature maps, then distinguishes two groups of mice with a CNN.
PUB2019· Sensors DOI PDF
Classification for Human Balance Capacity Based on Visual Stimulation under a Virtual Reality Environment
Describes walking posture with the triangle formed by upper, middle, and lower body centers of mass, and distinguishes balance ability via area variance, path, and gait speed.
PUB2018· Edutainment DOI
Barycentric Shift Model Based VR Application for Detection and Classification on Body Balance Disorders
Collects walking videos of two volunteer groups in a bridge-crossing VR scene and distinguishes abnormal balance ability via center-of-mass shift statistics.
PUB2017· Journal of Xi'an University of Technology
Human Action Recognition Based on Local Features in the Temporal Dimension
Human actions are recognized using local features along the temporal dimension.
