Research Directions

Large Models and Agent Applications
Focusing on large language models and agents, this direction studies task planning, tool use, and multi-agent collaboration, promoting application deployment, evaluation, and decision-making capabilities in vertical scenarios.
Low-Light Vision Enhancement
Addressing low illumination and complex lighting, this direction studies image/video enhancement, denoising, and feature representation, improving the usability of downstream tasks such as focusing, matching, and reconstruction under low-light conditions.
Depth Estimation
Addressing both standard and degraded scenarios, this direction studies depth estimation under monocular, low-light, and event-camera conditions, exploring semantic guidance, multimodal fusion, and robust 3D perception methods.
Human Action Recognition
Focusing on human behavior understanding, this direction studies skeleton-based and visual-feature-based action recognition methods, covering spatio-temporal modeling, robust representation, and behavior understanding applications in real-world scenarios.
