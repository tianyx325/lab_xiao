Contact & Admissions

Admissions

M.S. / Ph.D.

Computer vision, image processing, and artificial intelligence; rolling admissions.

  • Strong motivation and willingness to take on challenges
  • Solid mathematics foundation and English reading/writing
  • Good programming skills
  • Priority for combined B.S.–M.S. / B.S.–Ph.D. programs

Outstanding Undergraduates

Early-year students are welcome to join research and competitions.

  • Open to years 1–3
  • About 3–5 students per year
  • Participate in projects and competition coaching
  • Contact us by email with a short self-introduction

How to Apply

Send your materials to the advisor's email; we will reply as soon as possible.

  • Subject: Application inquiry – Name – University
  • Required: CV and transcripts
  • Optional: papers, projects, or competition experience
