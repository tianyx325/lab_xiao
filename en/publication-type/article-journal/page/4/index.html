Article-Journal

PUB2017· IEEE JSTSP DOI
Seeing Beyond Foreground Occlusion: A Joint Framework for SAP-Based Scene Depth and Appearance Reconstruction
Selects camera-array viewpoints via pixel clustering and recovers the depth and appearance of foreground-occluded scenes in a coarse-to-fine global optimization.
PUB2017· IEEE TIP DOI
Aliasing Detection and Reduction Scheme on Angularly Undersampled Light Fields
Locates aliasing regions caused by angular undersampling via spatial analysis during refocusing, and removes aliasing only in those regions.
PUB2017· Journal of Northwestern Polytechnical University
A Scene Depth-Independent Ghost Removal Algorithm for Virtual Aperture Images
To address ghosting artifacts in defocused regions of synthetic aperture photography images, an entropy-driven ghost removal method without depth estimation is proposed. By analyzing the formation mechanism of light field ghosts, entropy features are used to judge the focusing tendency and estimate a low-pass filtering kernel; ghosting is then suppressed by exploiting neighborhood pixel similarity and inter-frame continuity, while the image details in focused regions are preserved as much as possible.
PUB2017· Journal of Xi'an University of Technology
Human Action Recognition Based on Local Features in the Temporal Dimension
Human actions are recognized using local features along the temporal dimension.
PUB2016· Journal of Electronic Imaging DOI
Multispectral image compression methods for improvement of both colorimetric and spectral accuracy
Performs spectral–spatial clustering and PCA, compensates errors with visual-sensitivity weighting, then compresses multispectral images with conventional codecs.
PUB2015· Chinese Journal of Computers Paper
Multi-Cue Fused Depth Estimation Based on Light Field Analysis
Defocus and disparity cues are extracted from the light field captured by a camera array and adaptively fused; a Markov random field with adaptive smoothness constraints combined with graph cuts is then used to estimate globally consistent depth.
PUB2014· Application Research of Computers
A Video-Data-Driven Method for Generating Water Surface Models
An initial water surface is first recovered via shape from refraction, and a shallow-water simulation selected in a data-driven manner is then used as a physical constraint to reduce height-field jumps while maintaining temporal coherence.
PUB2014· Acta Automatica Sinica Paper PDF
Light Field Image Aliasing Detection Based on Randomly Masked Apertures
The coefficient of variation of the point set imaged through a randomly masked aperture is used to detect light field aliasing without requiring scene depth or texture priors, and the aliasing is then corrected according to the detection results.
PUB2014· Journal of Computer-Aided Design & Computer Graphics Paper PDF
A Multi-Scale Anti-Aliasing Rendering Method for Light Field Images
The relationship between angular undersampling and aliasing is analyzed, and gradient fusion of multi-scale light field images is used to reduce aliasing without requiring scene depth during rendering.
PUB2013· Journal of Northwestern Polytechnical University
A Depth Estimation Method Fusing Disparity and Focus-Zoom Approaches
A camera array is used to acquire multi-view data simultaneously; candidate depths are first provided by the disparity method and then fused with the zoom-based results through weighted linear blending to overcome weaknesses at depth discontinuities.
PUB2012· Computer Applications and Software
Depth Estimation Based on Confocal Image Sequences
The confocal image sequence synthesized from a camera array is treated as focus-defocus ranging data, and sharpness and color consistency are jointly used to evaluate focus for estimating the scene depth.
PUB2012· Journal of System Simulation
A Video-Based Underwater Scene Restoration Algorithm
Robust registration is first used to remove the geometric distortions caused by water-surface fluctuations, and the best image patches selected from the corrected video frames are then stitched together to recover the underwater scene.
