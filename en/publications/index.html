Reflectance oriented diffusion with normalizing flow illumination enhancement for the low-light images

Hang Yang · Zhaolin Xiao · Haonan Su

Enhances illumination with a normalizing flow, restores reflectance detail with a diffusion model, and corrects saturation after recombination, balancing enhancement and noise suppression.
2026· ICASSP DOI Code
FEDD-Net: A Frequency Diagonal Feature Enhanced Dual-Branch Diffusion Network for Low-Light Image Enhancement

Wengai Li · Zhaolin Xiao · Haonan Su · Qifeng Guo

Separates high/low frequencies with dual-tree complex wavelets, applies cross-attention across directions in the frequency domain to enhance diagonal features, and generates low-frequency structure and high-frequency detail via two diffusion paths.
2026· Multimedia Systems DOI
A dual-guide aware normalizing flow for low-light enhancement with global illumination and cross-channel attention

Xue Zhuang · Zhaolin Xiao · Haonan Su

Proposes a dual-guided normalizing flow that encodes illumination and color as separate conditions, mitigating uneven lighting and color distortion in low-light enhancement.
2026· Pattern Recognition DOI Code
FCRNet: Learning non-linear correspondences variation via a graph-based feature embedding for false correspondence removal

Ruiyuan Li · Zhaolin Xiao · Meng Zhang · Haiyan Jin · Haonan Su

Proposes the graph feature-embedding network FCRNet, which fits consistent and inconsistent true matches with learnable frequency responses to remove false correspondences.

Robust 2.5D Feature Matching in Light Fields via a Learnable Parameterized Depth-Degraded Projection

Meng Zhang · Haiyan Jin · Zhaolin Xiao · Jinglei Shi · Xiaoran Jiang

Uses the disparity of light-field Fourier disparity layers as coarse depth and learns a parametric depth-degradation projection to reduce depth ambiguity in feature matching.
