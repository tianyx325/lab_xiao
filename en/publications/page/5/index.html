2017· Journal of Northwestern Polytechnical University
A Scene Depth-Independent Ghost Removal Algorithm for Virtual Aperture Images

Zhaolin Xiao · 周果清

To address ghosting artifacts in defocused regions of synthetic aperture photography images, an entropy-driven ghost removal method without depth estimation is proposed. By analyzing the formation mechanism of light field ghosts, entropy features are used to judge the focusing tendency and estimate a low-pass filtering kernel; ghosting is then suppressed by exploiting neighborhood pixel similarity and inter-frame continuity, while the image details in focused regions are preserved as much as possible.
