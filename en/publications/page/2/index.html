Zhaolin Xiao · 苏展 · Fengyuan Zuo · Haiyan Jin

A latent feature-supervised Siamese network is proposed, in which normal-light motion features supervise the learning of low-light optical flow, avoiding the loss of motion information in the enhance-then-estimate pipeline.
