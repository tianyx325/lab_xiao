Zhaolin Xiao received his Ph.D. in Engineering.

**Position:** Professor, Doctoral Supervisor, and Vice Dean.

**Email:** [zhaolin.xiao@xaut.edu.cn](mailto:zhaolin.xiao@xaut.edu.cn)

**Academic Experience:**

His research focuses on computer vision, computational photography, and artificial intelligence. He works on light-field imaging analysis and 3D scene reconstruction theory, and has contributed to the development of several domestically developed light-field imaging systems with independent intellectual property rights. He is a Senior Member of CCF and a member of IEEE, as well as a member of the Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision technical committees of the Shaanxi Computer Federation, and the 3D Imaging and Display technical committee of the Chinese Society of Image and Graphics.

He has led 3 National Natural Science Foundation of China projects and 2 provincial-or-above research projects, was selected for the Shaanxi Overseas Returnees Science and Technology Activity Funding Program (Excellent Class), and participated in 1 key NSFC project as a sub-project leader. He has published more than 60 academic papers as first or corresponding author in leading conferences and journals such as CVPR, AAAI, IEEE TIP, IEEE JSTSP, PR, Chinese Journal of Computers, Acta Automatica Sinica, and Journal of Image and Graphics, and holds 15 authorized Chinese invention patents.

**Teaching Experience:**

He teaches courses including Introduction to Software Engineering, Software System Architecture and Framework, Fundamentals of Computer Science, and Scientific Paper Writing (graduate level). He has received honors at Xi'an University of Technology such as Outstanding Undergraduate Mentor, Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis Supervisor, Outstanding Young Teacher, and University-level Science and Technology New Star. He has guided students to over 30 competition awards, including a gold medal in the main track of the Shaanxi "Internet+" Competition and a second prize in the China Collegiate Programming Contest.

**M.S. / Ph.D. Recruitment:**

We continuously recruit M.S. and Ph.D. students in computer vision, image processing, and artificial intelligence. Desired qualities include a strong drive to improve, a solid foundation in mathematics and English, strong programming skills, optimism, resilience, and diligence. Students in combined bachelor's–master's or master's–Ph.D. programs are preferred.

**Undergraduate Recruitment:**

Outstanding undergraduate students in years 1–3 who are interested in research projects and academic competitions are welcome to contact me. I recruit 3–5 undergraduates each year.
