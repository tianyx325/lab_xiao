abstract: 'Salient object detection aims to quickly and effectively extract useful
  regions, i.e., object regions, from input images according to saliency features,
  and is essentially a segmentation task. High-quality salient object detection techniques
  can improve the ability of vision-perception-based artificial intelligence systems
  to judge and understand scenes. In the field of computer vision, salient object
  detection is an important branch that has gradually become a research hotspot, with
  broad application prospects in object recognition, image detection, image retrieval,
  and related industries. Traditional salient detection methods mainly target single-modality
  data in simple scenes; due to the low completeness of scene information, the theoretical
  extensibility of many stages, such as the analysis, extraction, representation,
  and computation of salient object features, is weak, and the scope of application
  is limited. Focusing on the theoretical research of salient object detection, this
  paper analyzes the main research directions and development status at home and abroad,
  summarizes the major applications of salient object detection, and discusses the
  current hot research topics as well as the key problems and challenges to be addressed
  in the future.
