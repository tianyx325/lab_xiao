abstract: 'To address the aliasing problem in existing light field images, a multi-scale
  anti-aliasing rendering method for light field images is proposed. First, the cause
  of image aliasing induced by angular downsampling of the light field is analyzed;
  then, a mathematical description of the relationship between the angular sampling
  rate and image aliasing is given, and the relevant factors affecting image aliasing
  are identified. On this basis, a method that fuses the gradients of multi-scale
  light field images is proposed to reduce image aliasing, with the advantage that
  no prior scene depth information is required. Anti-aliasing rendering experiments
  are conducted on both virtual light field data and real light field datasets, and
  the results show that the proposed method outperforms classical light field rendering
  methods and achieves results close to those of rendering methods that require known
  depth.
