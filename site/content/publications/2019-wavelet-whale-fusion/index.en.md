abstract: 'Image fusion technology can effectively improve the utilization ratio of
  image information, the accuracy of target recognition and the ability of image interpretation.
  In this paper, Whale Optimization Algorithm (WOA) based on the fusion techniques
  with multiscale geometric analysis is applied into image fusion. We use wavelet
  decomposition for image preprocessing, and then present an approach of image fusion
  based on wavelet with WOA. Experiment results demonstrate that the proposed method
  has advantages in subjectively qualitative analysis and objectively quantitative
  evaluation compared with the other state of the art methods.
