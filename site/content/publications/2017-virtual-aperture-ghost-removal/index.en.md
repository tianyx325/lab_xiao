  is proposed. By analyzing the formation mechanism of light field ghosts, entropy
  features are used to judge the focusing tendency and estimate a low-pass filtering
  kernel; ghosting is then suppressed by exploiting neighborhood pixel similarity
  and inter-frame continuity, while the image details in focused regions are preserved
  as much as possible.
