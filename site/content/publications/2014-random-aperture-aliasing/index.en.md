abstract: 'Existing light field acquisition systems suffer from undersampled angular
  information, and the resulting image aliasing degrades the quality of light field
  images. This paper analyzes the spatial-domain model of light field image aliasing
  within the 2D light field framework and proposes an aliasing detection method that
  varies the discrete aperture sampling density. The method performs aliasing detection
  by computing the coefficient of variation of the point set imaged through a randomly
  masked aperture (Random masked aperture, RMA), and its distinguishing feature is
  that no prior information about scene depth or texture is required. Using a planar
  camera array as the acquisition platform, the method is validated on multiple real
  light field datasets, and the aliasing effects are corrected on the basis of the
  detection results.
