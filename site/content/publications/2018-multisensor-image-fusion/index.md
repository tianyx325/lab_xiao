abstract: 'Multi-sensor image fusion is always an important and opening problem, which
  can enhance visual quality and benefit some social security applications. In this
  article, we use contrast pyramid to decompose visible and infrared images, respectively,
  and the directional filter banks are applied to obtain multiple directional sub-band
  image features. Then, we compute the decomposition coefficients of visible and infrared
  images using a low-pass filter on the decomposed data; and finally, we introduce
  the whale optimization algorithm to search optimal coefficients to reconstruct the
  final fusion image. The experiments are conducted on multiple datasets with subjective
  and objective comparisons, in which the qualitative and quantitative analyses indicate
  the validity of the proposed method.
