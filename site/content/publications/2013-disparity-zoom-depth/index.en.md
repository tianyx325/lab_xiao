abstract: 'The disparity method and the focus-zoom method are two image-based three-dimensional
  reconstruction approaches that possess different advantages and application scenarios
  owing to their differences in required datasets and depth computation. By exploiting
  a camera array to acquire multi-view datasets, a depth estimation method fusing
  the disparity and zoom methods is proposed. Candidate depth sets are first selected
  with the disparity method, and the final depth is then determined by a weighted
  linear blending function of the disparity and zoom results, which resolves the difficulty
  of dataset acquisition for the two methods as well as the low robustness of either
  single method in regions with depth discontinuities. Experimental results demonstrate
  the feasibility and superiority of the proposed method.
