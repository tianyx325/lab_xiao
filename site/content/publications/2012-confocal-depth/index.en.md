abstract: 'A depth estimation method based on confocal images is proposed. Virtual
  aperture techniques are employed to synthesize the multi-view images acquired by
  a camera array into confocal image sequences, which are then used as the data source
  for focus-defocus analysis to estimate scene depth. According to the characteristics
  of confocal images, a focus measure algorithm is proposed by combining the sharpness
  evaluation algorithm with the color consistency evaluation algorithm from traditional
  focus-defocus ranging methods, and this measure is applicable to focus analysis
  of both strongly and weakly textured regions in an image. Experimental results show
  that the proposed method is highly effective in focus discrimination and yields
  accurate scene depth estimation.
