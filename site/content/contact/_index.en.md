## Admissions

### M.S. / Ph.D.

Computer vision, image processing, and AI — applications welcome year-round.

- Strong motivation and resilience
- Solid math and English reading/writing
- Hands-on programming ability
- Integrated bachelor–master / bachelor–Ph.D. tracks preferred

### Outstanding undergraduates

Lower-year students are welcome for research and contests.

- Years 1–3
- About 3–5 students per year
- Research topics and contest mentoring available
- Email with a short self-introduction

### How to apply

Send materials to the advisor’s email; we will reply as soon as possible.

- Subject: Application inquiry — Name — School
- Required: CV, transcript
- Optional: papers, projects, or contest experience
